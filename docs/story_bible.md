# Lantern & Ladle — Story Bible

Characters, main story, quest lines with special customers, and dialog scripts.
Every quest is built from systems the prototype already has (named customers ordering through
the normal restaurant flow, pantry deliveries, floor/boss milestones, renown, the Festival), so
it can become data later (see **§5 System spec**).

**Tone:** cozy, warm, a little funny. Monsters are ingredients *and* neighbours. Nobody dies, nothing
is lost forever, and a missed visit just means "they'll come back tomorrow night".
**Idle rule:** stories never block play. Scenes appear as cards that don't pause the game, and
everything is kept in a Journal you can reread.

---

## 1. The world in one page

**Gloamhollow** is a market town built over **the Hollow**, a huge dungeon that "breathes" at dusk:
warm air rises, the lanterns flicker on, and what comes up from below is freshest right then.
So the town sleeps by day and eats by night — *what comes up at dusk is eaten by midnight.*

Above the market square hangs the **Great Lantern**, lit once a year at the **Festival of Lanterns**.
Folk say as long as it burns, the Hollow stays friendly. This year it's **dimming**, and nobody
knows why. (Answer: a blight in the Sunken Orchard is poisoning the Hollow's "breath".)

You inherit a shabby stall from **Grandma Odessa**, the market's most beloved cook, who has
gone travelling. She left you her cook Maro, a very eager adventurer named Pip, and a note.

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| The stall | Your restaurant. Grows from a counter with 3 stools into the heart of the market. |
| Night Market Street | Where customers queue. Other stalls, music, the rival Copper Wok across the way. |
| Mossy Cellars (F1–3) | Damp, mushroomy, full of rats. The Rat King hoards things that glitter. |
| Sunken Orchard (F4–6) | A drowned orchard of glowing fruit and huge bees. Something is wrong with the blossoms. |
| Lantern Temple | Brother Tull's home; where the Festival begins. |

## 2. Cast

Prototype visuals: emoji + body colour, like existing actors. **Special customers** get a gold
outline, their name under them, and a ❗ bubble when they have something to say.

### Your crew

**🍳 Maro — the cook** (starting chef)
- Big, quiet, gruff, secretly soft. Talks to his pots. Hums when a dish comes out perfect.
- Secret: twenty years ago he was an adventurer. On Floor 3 the Rat King's swarm nearly took
  his partner, and he's never set foot in the Hollow since. Odessa gave him a ladle instead of a sword.
- Voice: short sentences. *"Hm."* *"Taste first. Talk later."*
- Arc: from fear to pride. When the crew beats the Rat King, Maro cooks the Feast with his own recipe.

**🗡 Pip — the adventurer** (starting adventurer)
- Small, loud, fearless, terrible at maps. Names every rat they fight.
- Carries a brass **compass** (the 🧭 token in the dungeon strip!) that belonged to their mother,
  **Wren the Cartographer**, who vanished mapping the Sunken Orchard.
- Voice: exclamation marks, big plans. *"Floor THREE?! Finally!"*
- Arc: from wanting to be a legend to wanting to finish their mother's map — and finding her notes.

**✉ Grandma Odessa — the mentor (letters)**
- Never on screen in season 1; teaches through letters that arrive at milestones.
- Warm, sly, full of sayings. Signs every letter *"— O. (Don't let Maro skip lunch.)"*
- Arc: in season 3 she visits in person as a special customer, and asks you to cook *for her*.

## 3. Main story — *The Great Lantern*

Three acts, following the progression that already exists. Each chapter: **trigger → scene → steps → reward**.

### Act I — The Mossy Cellars

**Ch. 1 · A Shabby Stall** — *Day 1, on load*
> **✉ Odessa:** *"Dear one — the stall is yours now. The roof leaks, the stools wobble, and Maro will
> pretend he doesn't need you. He does. Feed the street, pay your people, and listen to the Hollow.
> — O. (Don't let Maro skip lunch.)"*

- Steps: serve 5 dishes.
- Reward: 50g · Journal unlocked.

**Ch. 2 · Something's Eating the Market** — *Day 2 dusk*
> **🧺 Hobb:** *"Three crates of rice, gone! Gnawed! The rats are bolder every week — they say they're
> fetching for some* king *down there."*
> **🗡 Pip:** *"A rat KING? Can I fight him? I want to fight him."*
> **🍳 Maro:** *"…No."*

- Steps: clear Floor 1 three times · serve Hobb a 🥩 Cellar Kebab ("turnabout is fair play").
- Reward: renown +5 · **Hobb's Discount**: rice costs nothing for the rest of the season.

**Ch. 3 · Maro Won't Go Down** — *Floor 3 unlocked*
> **🗡 Pip:** *"Maro! Floor three! You've been there, right? What's it like?"*
> **🍳 Maro:** *(stirring, not looking up)* *"Loud."*
> **🗡 Pip:** *"That's it?"*
> **🍳 Maro:** *"Dark. Teeth. A crown made of spoons."* *(long pause)* *"Bring everyone home."*

- Steps: none (story beat). Unlocks the Journal note *"Maro's partner"*.

**Ch. 4 · The Crown of Spoons** — *Rat King beaten*
> **🗡 Pip:** *"We WON! And — look what he was sitting on."* *(a dented ladle, engraved* M & R*)*
> **🍳 Maro:** *(very quiet)* *"…Rook's ladle. My partner. He dropped it running. So did I."*
> **🍳 Maro:** *"Hm. Tonight I cook the Feast. My way."*

- Steps: serve 3 🍱 Rat King's Feast (any customers).
- Reward: Maro +1 skill permanently (this season) · **Rook's Ladle** decoration on the PASS shelf.

### Act II — The Sunken Orchard

**Ch. 5 · The Dimming** — *Rat King beaten, next dusk*
> **🏮 Tull:** *"Forgive the intrusion. Look up — the Great Lantern. Do you see how it gutters? The
> Hollow breathes sick air, from somewhere below the Cellars. The old Orchard."*
> **🏮 Tull:** *"The guild will sell you a permit. It is expensive. Everything worth doing is."*

- Steps: buy the 🗝 Orchard Expedition Permit.
- Reward: renown +10 · Tull becomes a special customer (orders 🍲 Glowroot Soup, tips in candles).

**Ch. 6 · Wren's Map** — *first Floor 5 treasure room*
> **🗡 Pip:** *(holding a waterlogged notebook)* *"This is Mum's handwriting. "The blossoms are
> wrong. The bees are frightened, not angry. Something is feeding on the Queen.""*
> **🗡 Pip:** *"She was HERE. She was right here."*

- Steps: bring 10 🌸 Hollow Blossom of **Fine** quality to Tull (he studies the blight).
- Reward: Journal page *"Wren's Notes I"* · ⚔ Forged Gear lv.1 free.

**Ch. 7 · Frightened, Not Angry** — *Floor 6 unlocked; requires Moth line step 3*
> **🦋 Moth:** *(mask off for the first time — antennae, huge dark eyes)* *"She's my mother. The Queen.
> The blight makes her hurt, and when she hurts she stings. Please don't hurt her more than you have to."*
> **🍳 Maro:** *"…Then we cook something that helps."*

- Steps: serve Moth a 🧁 Blossom Honey Cake of **Good+** quality ("a taste of home, before the blight").
- Reward: party power +10% against the Hive Queen this season.

**Ch. 8 · The Queen's Court** — *Hive Queen beaten*
> **🗡 Pip:** *"She… stopped. She just sat down, and the blossoms went white again."*
> **🦋 Moth:** *"She's sleeping. Properly. For the first time in years."* *(hands you a jar)* *"Royal jelly.
> She'd want you to have it. For the Banquet."*
> **🗡 Pip:** *(quietly)* *"Mum's last page says: "If you find this, finish the map." I think I just did."*

- Reward: Journal page *"Wren's Notes II"* · Pip's compass needle stops spinning (token turns gold).

### Act III — The Festival

**Ch. 9 · Light It Again** — *Hive Queen beaten, next dusk*
> **🏮 Tull:** *"The air is clean. Now the Lantern needs an offering: the finest dish this market can make."*

- Steps: serve 1 🎂 Hive Queen's Banquet **to Tull** · hold the Festival of Lanterns.
- Scene on the festival card:
> *The whole street turns out. Sabine brings a wok of noodles, Vesna sells sparklers, Sir Bramblewick
> wears his old armour. Moth sits on Maro's shoulders. The Great Lantern catches — gold, then white —
> and for a moment the whole Hollow glows.*
> **✉ Odessa** *(a letter pinned to the stall the next morning)*: *"I saw it from three valleys away.
> Proud of you. Same time next year? — O."*

### Seasons 2+ — *Echoes*

After a festival the main story resets lightly: the same chapters run as **one-line reminders**
(no long scenes again), and each season adds one new **Echo** event:

| Season | Echo | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | *Royal Visit* | The Hive Queen visits as a special customer (huge, polite, orders three Honey Cakes, tips in royal jelly). |
| 3 | *Odessa Comes Home* | Odessa appears as a special customer and orders "whatever you're proudest of"; any 3★ dish ends the line with a final letter. |
| 4 | *Rook* | Maro's partner Rook turns up — alive, retired, running a tea stall two towns over. Hireable as a chef. |
| 5+ | *Lantern Tales* | A random Echo from a small pool (a lost tourist, a dragon who wants vegetarian food…). |

## 4. Side quest lines

Each line has 3–4 steps, a special customer who visits the stall, and a lasting reward.
Steps marked **[visit]** mean the character comes to the stall and orders that dish.

### 🛡 A Taste Remembered — *Sir Bramblewick*
A retired knight who sits on the same stool every night. His late wife Elsie ran a soup cart.

1. **[visit] 🍢 Mushroom Skewer** — *Day 2.*
   > *"Ah, a new cook! Elsie would've liked the look of you. Skewer, please. Easy on the salt — the
   > doctor says. Heavy on the salt — I say."*
2. **[visit] 🍲 Glowroot Soup, Good+** — *Chef Lv 2.*
   > *"Glowroot soup… she made it every Lantern Night. Hers had a green taste, like moss after rain."*
   > *(If served Common:)* *"Close. Not quite. I'll come back."*
3. **Deliver 3 🌿 Cellar Moss, Fine** — Maro wants to try Elsie's trick.
   > **🍳 Maro:** *"Fine moss. Dried, then crushed in. Old trick. Hm."*
4. **[visit] 🍲 Glowroot Soup, Fine+** — the remembered taste.
   > *(He eats in silence. Puts the spoon down.)* *"…That's it. That's hers."* *(He leaves something on the
   > counter: a battered shield.)* *"I don't need to remember the Hollow anymore. Your adventurers do."*

**Reward:** 🛡 *Bramblewick's Shield* — +2 power for every adventurer (forever, this save). Renown +15.

### 🦊 Fox's Bargains — *Vesna*
A fox-folk trader who buys low, sells high, and is honest about both.

1. **[visit] 🥩 Cellar Kebab** — *"Business is better on a full stomach. Who's your supplier? …Your
   supplier is a* child*? With a* sword*? Charming."*
2. **Deliver 10 🟢 Slime Jelly** — she pays 2× market value.
   > *"Up in the hill towns they call this "Hollow glass". They'll pay silly money. I adore silly money."*
3. **Deliver 5 🥕 Glowroot + 5 🍄 Cave Mushroom, Good+**
4. **[visit] 🍮 Slime Jelly Cup** — *"A toast. To partnership, and to markups."*

**Reward:** 🦊 **Vesna's Cart** opens as a new shop: buy dungeon ingredients for gold (stock refreshes
each dusk, prices rise with demand). **A real gold sink** that also patches ingredient shortages.

### 🪶 Three Tastings — *Lady Corvina Ashgrove*
The *Gloamhollow Gazette*'s critic. Arrives without warning, writes everything down, smiles never.

1. **[visit] any dish, Good+** — *"I am told this stall is "charming". I loathe charming. Impress me."*
2. **[visit] 🥘 Moss-Herb Stew, Fine+** — *"Hm. The moss is… intentional. Interesting."*
3. **[visit] 🍱 Rat King's Feast or 🥧 Lantern-Fruit Tart, Perfect** — waits a long time (patience ×3).
   > *(She sets down her pen.)* *"I came to write a short review. I'm afraid it's going to be a long one."*

**Reward:** 📰 *Gazette Review* — Nobles appear twice as often · renown +30 · the review is framed on the stall (Journal page).

### 🦋 Sweets for a Stranger — *Moth* (links to main Ch. 7)
A masked child who only appears in the last third of the night and pays in odd silver coins.

1. **[visit] 🍮 Slime Jelly Cup** — *(No words. A coin on the counter. A small bow.)*
2. **[visit] 🍮 Slime Jelly Cup, Good+** — *"…It's wobbly. I like wobbly."* *(first words)*
3. **[visit] 🧁 Blossom Honey Cake** — *Orchard open.* *"This tastes like… before. Before the bad flowers."*
4. → continues in **main Ch. 7–8**.

**Reward:** after Ch. 8, Moth sometimes "helps" at the stall: a free extra server on nights after a Hive Queen win (cosmetic flutter + real speed).

### 🥘 The Copper Wok — *Sabine*
The rival stall across the street. Competitive, generous, loud. Becomes a friend.

1. **[visit] her own order** — *"So you're the new kid. Grandma Odessa's stall, huh? Big apron to fill."*
2. **Cook-off #1** — *"Busiest night wins. Loser washes woks."* Serve **30 dishes** in one night.
   - Win: *"…Fine. FINE. Where do you get your rice?"* · Lose: *"Ha! Bring a sponge."* (retry next night)
3. **Cook-off #2** — serve **8 dishes of Fine+** in one night.
4. **[visit] 🍛 Orchard Boar Curry** — *"Okay. Okay. Truce. Teach me the curry and I'll teach you my
   wok trick."*

**Reward:** 🔥 *Wok Trick* — grill dishes cook 20% faster · Sabine joins the festival scene.

### 🧭 The Compass — *Pip* (personal, woven through main Ch. 3, 6, 8)
Small optional beats that fire as Pip levels:

| Trigger | Scene |
|---|---|
| Pip Lv 3 | *"I named the big rat Gerald. Gerald is my nemesis now."* |
| Pip Lv 5 | Pip asks to see the Journal map: *"Mum mapped the Cellars in a week. It's taking me a month."* — **Maro:** *"She didn't have to come home to cook."* |
| Pip Lv 8 | Pip practices with a wooden spoon. **Maro** corrects their grip. First time he talks about fighting. |
| Ch. 8 done | Compass token turns gold in the dungeon strip forever. |

### Crew barks (no quest, flavour)
Hired staff get one-line barks in the dive log or over their heads, by role and a random trait:

- *Night Owl server:* "Best part of the night is now." · *Clumsy chef:* "That was… meant to be on the floor."
- Adventurer after a win: "Rat stew, anyone?" · after a knockout: "I'm fine. The ceiling was in the way."

## 5. System spec (for implementation)

### 5.1 Data

```
data/defs/story_character_def.gd   # id, display_name, icon, color, title, is_special_customer
data/defs/quest_def.gd             # id, title, giver, line ("main" | "side"), chapter, prerequisites, steps, rewards, season_min
data/defs/quest_step_def.gd        # type, params (Dictionary), text, dialog_start, dialog_done, dialog_fail
data/defs/dialog_def.gd            # id, lines: Array[{speaker, text, emote}]
data/content/story/characters/*.tres
data/content/story/quests/*.tres
data/content/story/dialogs/*.tres  # or one dialogs.json per line, easier to write and translate
```

**Step types** — each one is a check against existing state, so steps also complete during offline catch-up:

| type | params | completes when |
|---|---|---|
| `serve` | recipe?, quality_min, count, scope (any/one_night) | dishes served (to anyone) |
| `visit` | customer, recipe, quality_min | that special customer is served that dish |
| `deliver` | ingredient, count, quality_min | auto-handed over at dusk if the pantry has it (setting: auto-deliver on/off) |
| `clear_floor` | floor, count | floor clear count reached |
| `beat_boss` | floor | boss in `bosses_beaten` |
| `buy_upgrade` | upgrade | upgrade level ≥ 1 |
| `reach` | stat (renown, chef_level, pip_level…), value | stat ≥ value |
| `night_record` | served or fine_served, value | a single night's report beats it |
| `festival` | — | festival held |

**Reward types:** gold, renown, ladles, recipe, upgrade level, perk-like modifier (`power_flat`, `traffic_noble`, `grill_speed`…), unlock (Vesna's Cart), decoration, journal page.

### 5.2 Special customers in the restaurant

- When an active step is `visit`, the giver is added to that night's spawn list (once per night, in the middle third).
- They skip the queue if a seat is free, order the step's dish if cookable (otherwise any dish + a hint
  line: *"No Glowroot tonight? Tomorrow, then."*), and have **3× patience**.
- Gold outline, name caption, ❗ bubble before ordering; served → dialog card → step check.
- **Missed visit = no penalty.** They come back next night. Idle-safe.
- The player can still click them to hand over a ready dish, like other customers.

### 5.3 Dialog presentation

- **Story cards** slide in over the restaurant (like the Dawn Ledger): portrait emoji + name, one line
  at a time, click or auto-advance (3 s per line at 1×, faster at higher speed). They never pause the game.
- During offline catch-up, scenes are queued and shown one after another on return
  ("While you were away… Sir Bramblewick stopped by").
- **Journal tab** (new): active quests with step progress, finished lines, replay any scene, Wren's notes,
  the Gazette review.

### 5.4 Save

`GameState.story = { quests: {id: {step, progress, done}}, seen_dialogs: [ids], journal: [ids] }`.
Main-line progress resets with the Festival (Echoes take over); side-line rewards marked
"forever" go into the legacy dictionary alongside ladles and perks.

## 6. Pacing map

Lined up against the current balance (bot run, days at 1× ≈ 8 min each):

```
Day  1  ┃ Ch.1 Shabby Stall ─ Odessa letter
Day  2  ┃ Ch.2 Rats · Bramblewick #1 · Hobb
Day  4  ┃ Bramblewick #2 (chef Lv2) · Sabine appears (renown 30)
Day  6  ┃ Moth #1 (first jelly cup) · Vesna appears
Day  9  ┃ Corvina #1 (renown 50) · Sabine cook-off #1
Day 10  ┃ Ch.3 Maro won't go down (Floor 3)
Day 12  ┃ Bramblewick finale → shield · Vesna's Cart opens
Day 16  ┃ Ch.4 Crown of Spoons (Rat King) · Ch.5 The Dimming
Day 17  ┃ Orchard opens · Moth #3
Day 20  ┃ Ch.6 Wren's Map (F5 treasure) · Corvina finale
Day 27  ┃ Ch.7 Frightened, not angry (F6)
Day 33  ┃ Ch.8 The Queen's Court · Sabine finale
Day 34  ┃ Ch.9 Light it again → Festival
```

Something story-ish lands every 1–3 days. The quietest stretch (days 20–27) is filled by the side lines.

## 7. Open questions

1. **Branching?** Everything here is linear. A light choice could fit Ch. 7 (fight the Queen vs. calm her with the cake first) — worth it?
2. **Names and pronouns** for Pip, Moth and Odessa are written neutrally / lightly gendered; adjust to taste.
3. **Portraits:** emoji for the prototype, or commission/paint simple bust portraits for story cards?
4. **Text volume:** ~120 lines in season 1. Fine for a prototype; voice-over or localisation would change how lines are stored (one JSON per line is easiest to translate).
